Hot Potato

A pass-the-phone party game for 3 or more.

How to play
  1. A category appears. Whoever holds the phone names something in it, then passes it on.
  2. No repeats, and no passing until you've said something.
  3. A hidden timer ticks down. Whoever is holding the phone when it explodes loses the round.
